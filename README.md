# NEXT5
NEXT5 is a personal prioritization and decision-support engine that filters daily chaos to answer one core question: "Given everything going on, what actually matters next?"

Behavioral Prioritization: Dynamically evaluates your confirmed goals, deadlines, available time, and real-time energy levels to recommend a maximum of five high-impact moves.

Earned Personalization: Learns exclusively from your observed behavior—what you complete, skip, or mark irrelevant—rather than making artificial AI assumptions or using pre-loaded personas.

Intelligent Boundaries: Designed to maximize meaningful progress over blind activity, it is uniquely capable of recommending that you set a boundary, step away from work, or prioritize recovery on a low-energy day.

Human-Driven Control: Organizes the noise from your natural-language brain dumps but requires your explicit confirmation before treating inferred context as reality.
